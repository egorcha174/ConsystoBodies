# Consysto Bodies Privacy Notice

**Effective date:** September 29, 2026

**Publisher:** Egor Chayka / Consysto

**Contact:** consysto@gmail.com

## Processing on your computer

Consysto Bodies works with the Autodesk Inventor documents you open and the output folders you select. It reads model geometry, model states, parameters and document properties to create or update parts and assemblies. Output folder preferences are saved as attributes in the master part. These attributes can include file paths and travel with the document when you share it.

The add-in writes local diagnostic logs. Logs can contain document names, file paths, model-state names and error details. The normal installed location is `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ConsystoAddin\bodies.log`. The add-in does not automatically send these logs or CAD documents to the publisher. No account or activation key is required. The add-in contains no analytics or advertising integration.

The installer stores the selected interface language and installation information in the current user's Windows profile. Uninstalling the add-in does not delete Inventor documents. You control generated files and local logs; review them before sharing them with anyone.

## Voluntary support requests

If you contact the publisher by email, Telegram or GitHub, the publisher receives the contact details, messages and files you choose to provide. They are used to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Do not send confidential CAD documents or third-party personal information without permission. Public GitHub issues are visible to other visitors.

Support communications and privately supplied diagnostic materials are kept only as long as needed to handle the request and for no longer than 24 months after the last interaction, unless a longer period is required to resolve a security issue or comply with law. Contact consysto@gmail.com to request deletion of support materials held by the publisher. Deletion from third-party platforms and their backups is subject to those platforms' processes.

## Third-party services

Autodesk Marketplace, GitHub, email and Telegram operate under their own privacy policies. Their handling of account information, downloads and messages is separate from the local operation of Consysto Bodies.
